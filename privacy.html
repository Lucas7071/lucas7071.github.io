Privacy Policy - Noobies Uploader

Noobies Uploader is used only by the channel owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channels. It requests YouTube access only to upload, schedule and manage the owner's own videos and playlists.

No data from other users is collected. The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared or sold. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
